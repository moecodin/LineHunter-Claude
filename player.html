    Where a player shoots, and how efficiently they shoot there, shows how they score. A big share of attempts at the rim points to a paint-and-finishing style, while a big above-the-break share points to a perimeter-shooting one. The colours compare each zone with the league average, so this is season-level context, not a prediction.
