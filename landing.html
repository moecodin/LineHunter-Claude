When does LineHunter launch?

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything available at launch is free and needs no card. A paid Pro plan comes later for advanced tools and strategies, and it will always be optional.

Can I place bets on LineHunter?

No. LineHunter is a research tool, not a sportsbook. We show stats, model scores and odds; any bet you place happens with your sportsbook.

Which sportsbooks do you show?

DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, ESPN BET, Fanatics, BetRivers, Hard Rock, BetParx and Bally Bet, where each one posts a line.

How does the Hunt Score work?

It starts from the player's season baseline, then adds or takes away points for minutes, recent form, the matchup, position matchup, pace and the book line. You see every step in the score build-up. It's still in training, and we're testing it against past NBA results.

What does a hit rate mean?

How many recent games a player went over tonight's line. 7/10 on 26.5 points means he scored 27 or more in seven of his last ten games.

Do I need to download an app?

No. LineHunter runs in your browser at linehunterapp.com, on your laptop or your phone.

Which sports are covered?

The NBA, all 30 teams. The NFL is next.

Is LineHunter available outside the US?

Not yet. LineHunter is built for users in the United States who are 21 or older.

Do you guarantee winning picks?

No, and nobody honest can. We give you better numbers; the decision is yours. Only bet what you can afford to lose.
