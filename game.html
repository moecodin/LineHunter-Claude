Model picks spread & total

Offensive rating is points scored per 100 possessions and defensive rating is points allowed. Each team's offense is paired with the other team's defense and scaled by both teams' pace, then home court, back-to-backs and injuries adjust each team's score. The spread pick and the total pick are the sides where the model's numbers beat the book's, and the strength is how far apart they are. Books watch the same things, so a big gap means take a closer look, not guaranteed.
